# VEGAN_AWARENESS_SOURCES.md — VeganSaathi

This document records the authoritative external references, health guidelines, and organizational definitions used across the VeganSaathi awareness module.

## 2. Editorial and Medical Guardrails

1. **Educational Only:** Nutritional information presented on VeganSaathi is for general educational awareness and community engagement. It does not constitute individual medical diagnosis, dietary prescription, or therapeutic treatment.
2. **Mandatory B12 Disclosure:** Every discussion of plant-based nutrition explicitly notes that Vitamin B12 is produced by micro-organisms, is absent from unfortified plant foods, and must be obtained via fortified products or supplements.
3. **Attributed Definitions:** The Vegan Society's definition is cited as an organizational definition, respecting that personal dietary choices and cultural realities vary.
4. **Honest Local Context:** In Shegaon and Maharashtra, traditional lacto-vegetarian food commonly uses ghee, curd, paneer, and milk sweets. VeganSaathi never assumes that a "pure veg" or "Jain" establishment is vegan unless specifically verified.
5. **Non-Judgmental Tone:** Guidance for students emphasizes realistic, affordable steps without purity tests or nutritional dogmatism.
