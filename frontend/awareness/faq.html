Student & Campus FAQ

Practical questions about campus meals, communicating with local vendors, nutrition, and dining out in Shegaon.

In smaller towns like Shegaon, the English word "vegan" may not be widely understood by roadside cooks. Instead of asking "Is this vegan?", ask directly about specific dairy ingredients using familiar local phrasing:

  • Hindi: "Bhaiya, isme ghee, butter ya dahi to nahi dala? Tel me banaya hai na? Roti pe ghee mat lagana."
  • Marathi: "Bhau, hyat tup kinva dahi ghatla ahe ka? He telat banavla ahe na? Chapati var tup nako lau."

Local cooks are generally very accommodating once they understand you simply want food cooked in plant oil (groundnut, sunflower, or soy oil) without butter or ghee.

Yes! Most student messes around SSGMCE operate on a staple rotation of dal, chapatis, seasonal sabzis, and rice. The main adjustments are:

  1. Request dry (sukhi) rotis directly from the tawa without ghee or butter.
  2. Ask if gravies use cream or curd. If so, choose dal and plain sabzis.
  3. Keep nutritious hostel snacks (roasted peanuts, roasted chana, bananas, sattu) in your room for extra energy between classes.

Vitamin B12 is essential. It is made by micro-organisms, not by plants. The NHS advises that sources of B12 for vegans are limited, so fortified foods or a supplement are needed.

Vitamin B12 supplements (cyanocobalamin or methylcobalamin) are sold in many pharmacies; a pharmacist or doctor can advise on the form and dose. Fortified plant milks and cereals also contribute. Speak with a doctor at the SSGMCE health center or a physician to determine an appropriate dosage.

Traditional Indian plant foods provide abundant calcium and iron when planned intentionally:

  • Calcium: Ragi (nachni / finger millet) is one of the richest sources of plant calcium in the world. Sesame seeds (til), dark green leafy vegetables (methi, moringa), and fortified soya drinks also supply bioavailable calcium.
  • Iron: Soya chunks, black chana, lentils, and spinach are rich in plant iron. To maximize absorption, pair them with Vitamin C by squeezing fresh lemon juice over your meals.
